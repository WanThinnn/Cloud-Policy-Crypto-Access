PQC Signature Setup

Your account needs a Post-Quantum Cryptography key to enable PQC Signatures for files. This uses secure biometrics (Fingerprint/FaceID/Screen Lock) to generate your signature keys.

Cross-Browser: If you created a key on another browser, you must use that browser, OR import your Recovery Phrase in the Keys Management page.

⚠️ Where to save: When prompted, you MUST select your device's built-in security:

  • Windows: Select "Windows Hello" (fingerprint/PIN/face)
  • macOS: Select "This Device" or Touch ID

❌ Do NOT select Google Password Manager, iCloud Keychain, or any Password Manager — they do not reliably support PRF encryption.

Enable Faster Login

Set up a login passkey to sign in quickly and securely using your fingerprint, face, or screen lock. No more passwords!

Save Your Recovery Phrase

Please download your 24-word recovery phrase. If you lose this file and your device, your files will be PERMANENTLY LOST.

Your recovery phrase has been generated securely on your device. We do not store this phrase.

Unlock PQC Signature Key

You need to unlock your PQC Key to continue. It will stay unlocked for 30 minutes of activity.

Recover PQC Signature Key

Enter your 24-word recovery phrase separated by spaces. This will recreate your PQC Signature Key and restore your access.
